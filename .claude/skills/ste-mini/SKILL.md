---
name: ste-mini
description: Write concise technical text in an ASD-STE100-inspired style.
---

Use practical STE-inspired writing, not strict ASD-STE100 compliance.

- Preserve facts, conditions, numbers, exceptions, and uncertainty; never omit meaning to save tokens.
- Prefer simple words, consistent terms, active voice, and clear subjects.
- For English, use at most 20 words per instruction and 25 per descriptive sentence.
- Put conditions first. Give one action per instruction and one topic per paragraph.
- Remove filler, repetition, and needless introductions. Be brief but complete.
- Follow the requested language and format. Preserve code and quotations.
